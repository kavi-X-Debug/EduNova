# Grade 6 ICT: curriculum analysis

## 1. Is ICT an official Grade 6 subject?
**Yes, under the current (pre-reform) curriculum.** ICT has been taught from Grade 6 to 9 since the 2018 syllabus. Official materials:
- EPD (edupub.gov.lk) lists a Grade 6 English-medium ICT **Reading Book** and **Workbook**. The site marks them "New Syllabus", but the books say first print 2018, second print 2019. "New Syllabus" means the 2018 ICT syllabus, not the 2026 reform.
- NIE (nie.lk, Teachers' Guides > English > Grade 6) lists "Information Communication Technology - ICT (2018)". This is the Teachers' Guide "Effective from 2018", and it includes the syllabus.

**2026 reform status.** The reform was meant to start Grades 1 and 6 in 2026 with new modules. Press reports say the junior-secondary framework keeps ICT as a compulsory subject, and that a Grade 6 ICT module was drafted and drew controversy in January 2026. In January 2026 Cabinet **deferred the Grade 6 reform to 2027** after a controversy over the English module; Grade 1 went ahead. In February 2026 the Ministry said the Grade 6 modules were finalised for rollout "next year".

As of 2026-11-13, neither edupub.gov.lk nor nie.lk has a reformed Grade 6 ICT module in any medium. The nie.lk syllabus list has no Grade 6 ICT entry.

**This path therefore uses the 2018 (pre-reform) materials.** Rebuild it when the reformed module is published, expected for 2027. The reform facts come from news reports (Daily FT 14 Jan 2026, Ada Derana, NewsFirst 24 Feb 2026), not from an official notice.

## 3. Competencies (TG syllabus, summarised)
The syllabus has six competencies and 14 competency levels, with 30 periods a year.

| 3 Uses operating systems | 3.1 handles files and windows (2) |
| 4 Uses application software | 4.1 mouse, keyboard and drawing (5); 4.2 audio and video software (2) |
| 5 Algorithms and simple programs | 5.1 problem solving as steps (2); 5.2 flowcharts (3) |
| 6 The Internet for information and communication | 6.1 Internet as a shared pool (3); 6.2 search engines (5); 6.3 safe and secure use (1) |

**Unit content (own words):**
1. **Importance of Computers.** The input-process-output model, and how computers compare with people on speed, accuracy, reliability, consistency, storage, cost and decision-making. Embedded computers in phones, washing machines, cars and TVs. The six hardware groups, hardware versus software with examples, and computers at work in schools, banks, hospitals, factories and agriculture.
2. **Computer lab safety.** Lab rules, the correct start-up and shut-down order, ethics, healthy posture, e-waste disposal, and passwords.
3. **Operating system and files.** What an OS does, using desktop icons, and window controls (minimise, maximise, resize, switch, close). Creating, saving, opening, editing and closing files and folders, using a drawing program.
4. **Application software.** Types of application software, mouse actions, keyboard key groups and finger placement, and drawing software. Then audio and video editing software, recording clips, and the ethics of recording people.
5. **Algorithms and flowcharts.** Problem solving as ordered steps, algorithms for daily tasks, the start/stop, input/output and process symbols, and sequential flowcharts.
6. **Internet.** What the Internet is, browsers (address bar and buttons), educational websites, search engines and search tips. Then safe use: usernames and passwords, e-mail safety, and safe online transactions.

## 5. Prerequisites
- computer-basics is the root.
- software-and-uses, safe-lab-use and problem-solving-algorithms depend on computer-basics. The algorithms concept is largely done without a computer.
- os-file-handling depends on software-and-uses and safe-lab-use.
- mouse-keyboard-drawing depends on os-file-handling, and audio-video depends on mouse-keyboard-drawing.
- flowcharts depends on problem-solving-algorithms.
- internet-search depends on os-file-handling and mouse-keyboard-drawing.
- online-safety depends on internet-search and safe-lab-use (which introduces passwords).

## 6. Assessment suggestions (TG, summarised)
The TG's assessment guidance is mostly practical:
- **1.x:** classify devices as input or output; time a computer against a person on a sum; list embedded devices at home; MCQ or structured questions; draw a simple picture; list school uses of computers.
- **2.x:** list lab good practices; start and shut down a computer; sketch a correct posture; find e-waste methods; explain why ethics and passwords matter.
- **3.1:** create, open, edit, save and close an image file.
- **4.1:** name mouse parts and actions; draw a given picture; sort keys into groups; do typing-tutor exercises.
- **4.2:** groups make an audio clip and use it in their own video.
- **5.x:** break a problem into steps; identify flowchart symbols; draw a flowchart.
- **6.x:** identify what is needed to go online; use educational sites; list and compare search engines; name three ways to protect data; explain why a trusted mentor matters.

**For the app:** quizzes can be MCQ or matching (device to category, symbol to meaning, key to group, safe or unsafe action). Hands-on outcomes such as drawing, recording, typing and browsing should be lab practice tasks the pupil confirms as done offline.

## 7. Gaps and uncertainties
- There is no reformed (2026/2027) Grade 6 ICT material on edupub.gov.lk or nie.lk. This path uses the 2018 syllabus and the 2019 reprint; rebuild it when the reformed module is released.
- The reform facts come from news reports and should be confirmed with moe.gov.lk or NIE.
- There is no standalone Grade 6 ICT syllabus on nie.lk; the syllabus in TG pp. 1-4 was used.
- The Chapter 3 section titles are images in the PDF, so that content was inferred.
- The Chapter 4 split (RB pp. 41-50 versus 51-54) is placed at the start of the audio/video editing subsection.
- WB Activity 1.4 has no explicit link in the textbook; it was assigned to software-and-uses by its position.
- Two concepts span two terms and are set to the term holding most of their periods. mouse-keyboard-drawing is set to Term 2 (1 of 5 periods is in Term 1). flowcharts is set to Term 2 (1 of 3 periods is in Term 3).
- The Sinhala and Tamil books are recorded as links only. The NIE Sinhala and Tamil guide lists were not resolved, so title_si is omitted.
- The 2018/2019 content is somewhat dated (optical discs, named software). Lessons may need light modernising, keeping the same outcomes.
