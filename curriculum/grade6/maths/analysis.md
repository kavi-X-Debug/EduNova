## Competencies and competency levels (summarised from guide ch. 1, pp. 1-17)

- **1 Operations on numbers for daily needs.** 1.1 place value up to billions; 1.2 number line, negative numbers and integers; 1.3 comparing and ordering integers; 1.4 adding and subtracting whole numbers; 1.5 multiplying and dividing by 10/100/1000 and two-digit numbers; 1.6 factors and multiples up to 100; 1.7 divisibility by 2, 5, 10; 1.8 estimating collections; 1.9 rounding below 100 to the nearest ten.
- **2 Relationships between numbers.** 2.1 odd/even, prime/composite, square and triangular numbers; 2.2 simple number patterns.
- **3 Parts of a unit.** 3.1 unit and proper fractions; 3.2 equivalent fractions; 3.3 comparing fractions; 3.4 adding/subtracting fractions with equal or related denominators; 3.5 recognising and comparing decimals to 2 places; 3.6 adding/subtracting decimals.
- **4 Ratios.** 4.1 ratio, equivalent ratios, simplest form, simple rates.
- **6 Indices.** 6.1 index notation; numbers below 100 as powers; expanding powers.
- **7 Length and perimeter.** 7.1 units mm/cm/m/km, measuring, converting, estimating; 7.2 perimeter of rectilinear figures.
- **8 Area.** 8.1 area as surface extent, arbitrary units, cm², squares and rectangles on a 1 cm grid.
- **9 Mass.** 9.1 g and kg, conversion, adding and subtracting.
- **11 Liquid measures.** 11.1 ml and l, conversion, estimation, adding and subtracting.
- **12 Time.** 12.1 units, elapsed time, timetables; 12.2 12- and 24-hour clock, dates as yyyy.mm.dd.
- **13 Directions.** 13.1 eight directions, relative location, horizontal and vertical.
- **14 Algebra.** 14.1 symbols for unknowns and variables; 14.2 one-unknown expressions with + or - and substitution.
- **21 Angles.** 21.1 classifying angles against a right angle.
- **22 Solids.** 22.1 nets and models of cube, cuboid, regular tetrahedron; faces, edges, vertices; compound solids.
- **23 Plane figures.** 23.1 features of rectangle, square, triangle, parallelogram, trapezium; drawing on a grid.
- **24 Circles.** 24.1 circular objects and designs without compasses.
- **28 Data collection.** 28.1 tally marks, tables, picture graphs.
- **29 Data interpretation.** 29.1 reading tables and picture graphs.
- **30 Sets.** 30.1 sorting a collection by a common property.

## Prerequisites between units

Textbook order is the backbone. Extra dependency links used in `path.json`:
- Place Value before Whole-Number Operations, the Number Line and Decimals.
- Number Line before Estimation and Rounding.
- Whole-Number Operations before Time, Fractions, Factors and Multiples, Length, Liquids, Mass, Algebraic Symbols and Indices.
- Angles before Directions and Rectilinear Plane Figures.
- Fractions before Decimals and Ratio.
- Factors and Multiples before Types of Numbers and Ratio.
- Types of Numbers before Indices.
- Rectilinear Plane Figures before Solids and Area; Length before Area.
- Selection before Data Collection, which comes before Data Interpretation.
- Algebraic Symbols before Constructing Expressions.

## Assessment suggestions (from the guide's notes, own words)

- Lesson plans carry assessment criteria for the target skill and for attitudes and group work. App equivalent: a short checklist quiz on the skill plus optional self-reflection prompts.
- The guide sends students to the matching textbook exercise after each lesson. The app's practice step should use original items of the same type and difficulty; textbook items must not be copied.
- Lessons favour discovery and group activities with hands-on materials; lesson steps can use interactive versions (draggable number line, grid drawer, net folder).
- Revision Exercises 1-3 close each term and fit a mixed end-of-term quiz.
- Some levels (e.g. 1.5, 12.2, 2.2, 7.2) have no specimen plan or assessment criteria in the guide, so their app items need their own design.

## Mapping to the app path (`path.json`)

- 25 concepts, one per textbook lesson, in textbook order. Revision exercises are not separate concepts; each concept has its own revision step.
- Multi-level units (Number Line, Fractions, Decimals, Time) were kept whole.
- Step minutes scale with periods: lesson about 10 + 2.5 x periods (max 40); practice about 10 + 2 x periods (max 30); quiz and revision 10 each.
- `title_si` is omitted: the Sinhala-medium book was not downloaded and the English book's Sinhala glossary uses a legacy font.

## Gaps and uncertainties

1. **nie.lk was not reachable by script** (Cloudflare challenge). The NIE guide and syllabus came from the Ministry of Education's e-thaksalawa site (moe.gov.lk).
2. **Reform module not obtained.** The only lead is a Channel NIE (nie.ac.lk) link that could not be fetched.
3. **The 2007 syllabus is outdated**; downloaded but ignored.
4. **Some guide page ranges are approximate.**
5. **Part I contents pages are images**; unit ranges were found from lesson-opening pages.
6. **Small labelling differences**: "Selecting" vs "Selection", "Ratio" vs "Ratios".
7. **edupub HTTPS timed out**; textbooks were fetched over plain HTTP from the same official host. Checksums are in `manifest.json`.
